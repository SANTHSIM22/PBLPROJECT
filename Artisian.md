# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Artisan Connect Platform

### 1. Product Overview

*Product Name:* Artisan Connect Platform  
*Version:* 1.0.0  
*Product Type:* Web (Backend API + Fronte
nd Interface)

Artisan Connect Platform is a digital marketplace designed to bridge the gap between *local artisans* and *customers* through a multilingual, accessible, and interactive platform.  
It allows artisans to showcase handmade crafts, share cultural stories, manage sales and payments, while customers can discover, purchase, and engage with authentic handmade products.  
Admins oversee platform activities, user verification, and content moderation.

### 2. Target Users

- *Artisans:* Create profiles, list products, share craft stories, and manage orders and earnings.  
- *Customers:* Browse, purchase, and review handcrafted products.  
- *Administrators:* Verify artisans, manage platform content, monitor transactions, and handle reports.

### 3. Core Features

#### 3.1 User Authentication & Roles

- *Signup/Login System:* Email or username, passowor
- *Role-Based Access:* Artisan, Customer, and Admin.  
- *Profile Management:* Edit personal details, change passwords, manage profile photo and contact info.  


#### 3.2 Artisan Features

- *Profile Setup:*  
  - Add craft type, location, bio, and portfolio images.  
- *Product Management:*  
  - Add, edit, delete crafts with images, videos, material details, and pricing.  
  - Manage stock and availability status.  
- *Story Behind the Craft:*  
  - Record or upload short video to share cultural stories.  
- *Order Dashboard:*  
  - Track order status (Pending, Shipped, Completed).  
- *Earnings Overview:*  
  - View total sales, income, and payouts.  
 

#### 3.3 Customer Features

- *Registration/Login:* 
- *Homepage Discovery:*  
  - Search by craft type, region, or artisan name.  
  - Category-wise browsing (pottery, textiles, jewelry, etc.).  
- *Product Details Page:*  
  - View product images, price, description, and artisan’s story.  
- *Cart & Checkout:*  
  - Add multiple items, adjust quantities, and process secure payments.  
- *Order Tracking:*  
  - Track order journey from “Placed” → “Shipped” → “Delivered.”  
- *Wishlist & Favorites:* Save preferred crafts for later purchase.  
- *Review & Rating System:*  
  - Leave feedback for both products and artisans.  
- *Support System:*  
  - In-app chat, FAQ, and AI-based chatbot for assistance.  

#### 3.4 Admin Features

- *Admin Dashboard:* Overview of total artisans, customers, and orders.  
- *Artisan Verification Panel:*  
  - Approve or reject artisan registration and listings.  
- *Product Management:*  
  - Edit or remove inappropriate listings.  
- *Order Monitoring:*  
  - Track transactions, delivery delays, and disputes.  
- *Reports & Analytics:*  
  - Generate reports for total users, revenue, and product trends.  
- *CMS (Content Management System):*  
  - Manage homepage banners, blog posts, and featured artisans.  
- *Feedback & Complaint Handling:*  
  - Manage reported users or issues.  

#### 3.5 Additional & Enhancement Features

- *Responsive Design:* Compatible with web and mobile devices.  
- *Accessibility-Friendly UI:* Large icons, minimal text, 
- *Search, Filter, and Sort Options:* By price, rating, popularity, or location.  
- *Promotions & Highlights:* Featured artisans and products.  
- *Social Media Sharing:* WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook integration.  
- *Newsletter Subscription:* For updates and artisan stories.  
- *Training Module for Artisans:* In-app tutorials on uploading and managing crafts.
